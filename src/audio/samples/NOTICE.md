# Guitar, bass and piano samples

The `.mp3` files in this folder are the "Acoustic Guitar (steel)" instrument from the
FluidR3_GM soundfont, as rendered to per-note MP3s by the
[midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) project
(`FluidR3_GM/acoustic_guitar_steel-mp3/`).

Attribution: FluidR3_GM soundfont by Frank Wen; MP3 rendering by Benjamin Gleitzman
(midi-js-soundfonts). The app credits this in its footer and on its About and licences screen.

The ten files in `bass/` (E1 to G3, a minor third apart) are the "Electric Bass (finger)"
from the same soundfont and rendering (`FluidR3_GM/electric_bass_finger-mp3/`), same licence
and attribution. They play everything when the learner chooses the bass.

The four files in `piano/` (G2, G3, E4 and E5) are the "Acoustic Grand Piano" from the same
soundfont and rendering (`FluidR3_GM/acoustic_grand_piano-mp3/`), same licence and
attribution. They are used only in the tutorial screens that compare the guitar (E4, E5) or
the bass (G2, G3) with the piano.

Only the notes the app needs are included. Tone.js pitch-shifts from the nearest
sample for anything in between.
