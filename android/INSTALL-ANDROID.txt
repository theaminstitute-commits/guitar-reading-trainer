GUITAR READING TRAINER for Android
==================================

GuitarReadingTrainer-v<version>.apk

1) Copy the .apk to the phone (Drive app, USB, or open the Drive link on the phone).
2) Tap it. Android asks to allow installs from this source the first time; allow it.
3) Tap Install. Guitar Reading Trainer appears in the app list with a staff icon.

A newer .apk installs over the old one and keeps your progress. The app works
offline: the guitar sounds and music fonts are inside it, and nothing leaves
the phone. It is signed with a test key, so Play Protect may ask once whether
to install anyway.

The same app also runs in the phone's browser at
https://theaminstitute-commits.github.io/guitar-reading-trainer/ and can be
added to the home screen from the browser menu.
