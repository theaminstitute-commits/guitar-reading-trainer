GUITAR READING TRAINER for Linux Mint / Ubuntu - pick ONE of these
===================================================================

1) guitar-reading-trainer_0.3.1_all.deb   (recommended)
   Double-click it. Mint's Package Installer opens: click "Install Package"
   and enter your password. Guitar Reading Trainer then appears in the menu
   under Sound & Video and Education.
   Remove later with:  sudo apt remove guitar-reading-trainer

2) GuitarReadingTrainer.run   (no install)
   Right-click > Properties > Permissions > tick "Allow executing file as
   program". Then double-click it and choose "Run".
   (Google Drive downloads always lose that permission, so this step is needed once.)

3) GuitarReadingTrainer-linux.zip
   The same app as a folder with install.sh (per-user, no password).

All three open the app in its own window when Chromium or Google Chrome is
installed (sudo apt install chromium); otherwise it opens in Firefox.
Works offline. Your progress stays on this computer.

The same app also runs on a phone from
https://theaminstitute-commits.github.io/guitar-reading-trainer/
